3 · “Separate genuine rejections from avoidable losses”

Four ways a user goes unverified

Genuine: properly assessed, correctly declined. Avoidable: could plausibly have verified, lost to process. Rule: 2+ checks, all failed → genuine; one check, never routed further → avoidable.
